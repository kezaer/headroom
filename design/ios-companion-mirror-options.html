The companion and the widget are separate designs. Left is today's app. The middle and right pull the widget's language into the app to different degrees. Both mirrored versions drop the “Plan renews” row when there's no renewal date (per your call), and swap the white name + colored plan capsule for the widget's accent-colored name + quiet gray pill.

The real decision is how far to go: a full mirror (compact, widget-identical, quiet) or a hybrid that keeps the app's “Usage” hero and “% left” clarity but adopts the widget's tile styling.

My read

Go Hybrid. The app has room the widget doesn't, so a full mirror leaves it feeling sparse and throws away genuinely useful affordances — the “Usage” hero orients you, “% left” is unambiguous, and the Low chip flags trouble at a glance. The Hybrid keeps those while adopting the widget's cleaner tile identity (accent name, quiet pill) so the two read as one family without dumbing the app down to a big widget.

Either way I'd also unify the window order — the widget currently lists Weekly-then-Session while the app lists Session-then-Weekly. I've shown Session-first everywhere here (the more urgent window first); we should pick one and apply it to both.
