The problem

The large widget shows the same as the medium — just taller.

Today the large renders two providers × two windows and drops all the leftover height into a single Spacer at the bottom. Nothing scales to occupy it, so ~35% of the canvas reads as accidental empty space.

Two honest levers: give the existing four meters room to breathe, or show more real data — Codex's extra spark limits (its additional_rate_limits) that the Mac already parses but the widget currently discards. A note on that lever: only Codex has spark windows — Claude doesn't, so bringing them back is a deliberate asymmetry, not a symmetric add. Three directions below.

B · Full Picture

Restores Codex spark

Fills the space with meaning, not just air. Codex's spark limits come back as a clearly subordinate Additional limits group under its primary two — smaller, muted, thinner bars. Claude keeps its two (it has no spark), and the asymmetry now reads as intentional: Codex simply has more to report. This is the literal answer to “should we bring the spark readouts back.”

C · Provider Tiles

Layout + surfaces

Gives the large its own identity instead of being a stretched medium. Each provider becomes an elevated tile — the ring-shadow / light-glow elevation from the companion app — and the two tiles flex to fill the canvas with structure and a real gap. Echoes the Mirror direction (two provider tiles, whole picture always). Spark can optionally live inside the Codex tile.

  • Two tiles, equal flex — the gap does the space-filling
  • Elevation via ring-shadow (light) / light-glow (dark), no hard borders — the Jakub approach, shared 1:1 with the iOS companion app
  • Concentric radii: 12px widget inset so 14px tiles stay concentric
  • Header fix: title band now gets ~15px above and ~16px below (vs the tile-to-tile 10px) — it reads as its own zone, not squeezed onto the first tile
  • App icon: the vessel mark anchors the header and balances “just now.” Kept small and on the app's own mark so it never reads as a third provider next to Claude / Codex

Why no app icon — decided

Resolved

Kept for the record. The tension was never “is that a third provider” — nobody reads the vessel as an AI. It's that the app mark and the two provider marks are the same rounded-square silhouette at the same size, stacked down the left edge, so for a beat they read as one set of three. We went with no icon — cleanest and most native. The treatments below show the alternatives that were considered.

Where this lands

Option C · Provider Tiles, no app icon. Ring-shadow / light-glow elevation (the Jakub approach), two equal-flex tiles, concentric 14px radii, and the roomier header band. No header glyph — the wordmark names the app and Apple's own widgets skip it, so it's the cleaner, more native call and avoids competing with the provider marks.

Header: title up to 19px, leading-aligned (not centered — centering fights the left-aligned tiles and reads less native), and the timestamp retired to a quiet footer, right-aligned — it sets up a diagonal (title top-left ↔ timestamp bottom-right) and shares the right edge with the % and Renews metadata. Label is a micro-choice: Updated just now is cleanest; from Mac · just now keeps the mirror-provenance cue.

Mirror it 1:1 on the iOS companion app so the widget and the phone app share one visual language.

Rendered at ~340×356 (iPhone large family, approx). Health colors drive the bars; brand color touches only identity. Toggle the surface to check both themes. Values match the 2026-07-04 screenshot.
